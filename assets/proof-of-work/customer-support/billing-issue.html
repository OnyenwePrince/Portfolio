Case Study 01

Duplicate Billing Charge Resolution

Company Acme Software Ltd.
Ticket ID #10452
Customer Sarah Johnson
Support Agent Prince Onyenwe
Status Refund Processed
Resolution Time 15 Minutes

Customer Conversation

SJ
Sarah Johnson
Customer
Hello, I was reviewing my bank statement this morning and noticed I was charged twice for my monthly subscription. I only completed the payment once, so I'm concerned that I may have been billed in error. Could you please investigate this and let me know how the duplicate charge can be resolved? Thank you.
PO
Prince Onyenwe
Customer Support Specialist
Hello Sarah, Thank you for bringing this to our attention. I sincerely apologize for the inconvenience. After reviewing your billing history, I confirmed that a duplicate transaction was processed because of a temporary payment gateway timeout. I've successfully initiated a full refund for the duplicate payment. The refund has been approved and should reflect in your account within 3–5 business days, depending on your financial institution. I've also reported the transaction to our billing team to prevent similar issues in the future. Thank you for your patience, and please don't hesitate to reach out if you need any further assistance. Kind regards, Prince Onyenwe Customer Support Specialist

Resolution Summary

  • Verified duplicate payment in the billing system.
  • Identified payment gateway processing error.
  • Initiated a full refund immediately.
  • Explained the refund timeline to the customer.
  • Escalated the gateway issue to the billing team for investigation.
