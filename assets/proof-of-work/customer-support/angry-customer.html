Emma Wilson

Hello Emma, Thank you for reaching out, and I'm sincerely sorry for the frustration you've experienced. I completely understand why you're upset, especially after contacting us multiple times without a resolution. I've personally reviewed your order and found that it was delayed because of a courier processing error. I've arranged for an immediate priority shipment at no additional cost and also added a service credit to your account as an apology for the inconvenience. You'll receive a tracking email within the next few minutes. Thank you for giving us the opportunity to make this right. Kind regards, Prince Onyenwe Customer Support Specialist

Resolution Summary

  • Customer's concerns acknowledged with empathy.
  • Order delay investigated immediately.
  • Priority shipment arranged.
  • Compensation applied to the customer's account.
