Case Study 06

Subscription Cancellation & Customer Retention

Company Acme Software Ltd.
Ticket ID #10702
Customer Sophia Adams
Support Agent Prince Onyenwe
Status Retained
Resolution Time 14 Minutes

Customer Conversation

SA
Sophia Adams
Customer
Hello, I'd like to cancel my subscription. I haven't been using the platform much recently, and I don't think it's worth continuing with the monthly payments. Please cancel my subscription immediately.
PO
Prince Onyenwe
Customer Support Specialist
Hello Sophia, Thank you for reaching out. I completely understand your concern. Before processing the cancellation, I wanted to let you know that we recently introduced a lower-cost subscription plan that includes the features most customers use regularly. Based on your account activity, I believe this plan would better suit your needs while reducing your monthly cost. After reviewing the available options together, you chose to switch to the lower plan instead of cancelling completely. I'm glad we found a solution that works for you. Thank you for remaining with us. Kind regards, Prince Onyenwe Customer Support Specialist

Resolution Summary

  • Listened carefully to the customer's concerns.
  • Identified the reason for cancellation.
  • Recommended a more suitable subscription plan.
  • Reduced the customer's monthly cost.
  • Successfully retained the customer.
